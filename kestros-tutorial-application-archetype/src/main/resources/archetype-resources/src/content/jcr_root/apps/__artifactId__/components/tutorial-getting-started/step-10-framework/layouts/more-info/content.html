Step 10

Services, from the more-info layout

Both of this framework's layouts read the same model, and neither of them knows where the data came from. That separation is the whole reason a component can carry several views.

Where each piece lives

  • api/src/main/java/.../services - the interface, and any exception it declares.
  • core/src/main/java/.../services - the implementation, and its unit test.
  • application/src/main/java/.../components - the Sling Model the view binds to.

Things worth knowing early

  • A model that cannot get its service should render nothing rather than fail the page - the rest of the page is not at fault.
  • Services are singletons in the container. Anything per-request belongs on the model, not on the service.
  • Kestros's health checks will report a service that never came up, which is usually a missing OSGi annotation rather than a mistake in the logic.
