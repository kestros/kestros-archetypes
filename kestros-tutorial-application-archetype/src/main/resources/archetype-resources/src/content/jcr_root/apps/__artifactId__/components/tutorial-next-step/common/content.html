Where to go from here

The tutorial ends at step 11. This component is the part that outlasts it - drop it on a page of your own and it keeps the reference links to hand while you build.

The three sites this project generated

  • Beginner - the eleven-step walk you have just finished.
  • Intermediate - the same component, yours to re-theme as practice.
  • Advanced - a third copy, for trying something that would break the other two.

The parts of your codebase the tutorial touched

  • src/content/jcr_root/apps/${artifactId}/components - component types and their views.
  • src/content/jcr_root/etc/ui-frameworks - frameworks, their versions and their themes.
  • src/content/jcr_root/etc/vendor-libraries - libraries, and the CSS and JavaScript they carry.
  • src/content/jcr_root/content/sites - the sites themselves, and the kes:theme that decides which framework renders each one.

Generated documentation
